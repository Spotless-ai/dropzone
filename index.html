      Privacy, limits and preview status

      The selected files are processed in memory on this device. This preview has no upload endpoint, account system, analytics, external fonts or CDN scripts. Loading the app still makes ordinary requests to its host. No offline caching is included yet.

      Image conversion redraws the picture and does not copy original EXIF, GPS, IPTC or XMP metadata. This is not a guarantee of anonymity: visible information stays in the picture, and encoders can add their own technical metadata. Animated images, SVG, HEIC and RAW formats are not supported.

      Limits: 100 files, 100 MB total input, 25 MB per image, 24 megapixels per image, and 100 MB of generated outputs. Your browser may run out of memory below these limits. Cancel stops the processing worker; closing or reloading clears the selection and outputs.

      This is an unpublished browser preview, not the desktop installer. Video conversion and ZIP extraction are not included. It has not completed cross-browser release testing.
